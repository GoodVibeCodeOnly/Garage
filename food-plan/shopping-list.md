## What the list gives you

**About 125 g of protein and 40 g of fibre a day** if you ate all of it over seven days. You won't eat all of it, and that's the point. **Even eating three-quarters of it still hits both targets.**

- **The 12 shakes last about a week and a half.** The leftovers cover part of next week, so next week's trip is cheaper.
- **If money's extra tight:** a 6-pack of shakes instead of 12 saves about $6. Lean on the chicken and beans that week.
- **If you have $5 more:** add peanut butter ($2, shelf-stable backup protein) and a box of raisin bran ($2.50, 7 g fibre a cup).

## When you get home (5 minutes, once)

1. **Pull the chicken off the bones into a container.** Date the lid. If it's more than you'll eat in 4 days, freeze half.
2. **Shakes in the fridge, front and centre.**
3. **Bananas and apples somewhere you can see them.**
