Every day, roughly

  1. 1 shake
  2. 1 meat thing (sandwich, chicken, dogs)
  3. 1 bean thing (beans, chili, lentil soup)
  4. 2 pieces of fruit
  5. Milk or kefir somewhere

That's ~90 g protein and ~30 g fibre. No counting needed.

How long it keeps (fridge)

Rotisserie chicken (fridge within 2 h)3–4 days
Opened deli meat3–5 days
Opened hot dogs (heat till steaming)1 week
Opened milk / chocolate milk~7 days
Opened can (beans, chili, soup)3–4 days
Frozen meat at 0°Fsafe; best in 1–6 mo

Sour smell or mould: toss it. When in doubt, throw it out.

Not hungry?

  • Can't face food: drink a shake slowly or a glass of chocolate milk.
  • Queasy: something cold and plain: kefir, banana, dry cereal.
  • Hungry in the evening? That's catch-up time: sandwich, chili dogs, whole can of soup.
  • Water: a glass with every item on this sheet. Stimulants hide thirst.
