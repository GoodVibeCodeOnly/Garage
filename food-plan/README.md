# Food plan

**Targets: about 90 g protein, 30 g fibre and plenty of water a day.** No cooking, a microwave at most, and two minutes or less of your time per item. Built around the food you already like.

**Start here:** print **[fridge.html](fridge.html)** and stick it on the fridge door. It's the whole guide on one page. Open it in a browser, then Ctrl+P / Cmd+P. It's sized for US Letter.

| File | What's in it |

| [not-hungry.md](not-hungry.md) | What to eat when you don't feel hungry but need to, plus water |
| [sample-days.md](sample-days.md) | Three sample days that hit the targets |
| [shopping-list.md](shopping-list.md) | One trip, one week, ≈ $58 with tax (estimates) |
| [food-safety.md](food-safety.md) | How long chicken, deli meat, hot dogs, milk and opened cans last; freezing |
| [fridge.html](fridge.html) | One printable page for the fridge door |
| [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md) | Where every number came from |

**The short version:** one shake, one meat thing, one bean thing, two pieces of fruit, and milk or kefir somewhere. A glass of water with each. That's the day.
