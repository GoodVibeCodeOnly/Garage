# Not hungry, but you need food

**Appetite and need aren't the same thing.** Pick the row that matches right now.

| Right now you… | Eat / drink this | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Can't face food at all** | A protein shake, sipped over 20–30 min. Or a cup of chocolate milk. | Drinking takes no appetite, and one shake is a third of the day's protein. |
| **Have about 30 seconds of willingness** | Shake, banana, string cheese, or chicken straight from the container | No plate, no prep and no decision. |
| **Can manage a few bites** | A handful of dry Cheerios, a banana, or a few pieces of cold chicken | Small and plain. Stop whenever you like; it still counts. |
| **Feel a bit queasy** | Something cold and bland: kefir, banana, dry cereal | Cold, plain food sits easier than hot or greasy. |
| **Want something warm** | A mug of lentil soup or chili, 90 s in the microwave | Warm, soft and filling. Soup also counts as water. |
| **Have a dry mouth** | Kefir, chocolate milk or soup | Wet food goes down easier than a dry sandwich. |
| **Hungry for once** (often evening, as the dose wears off) | The big stuff: two-meat sandwich, chili dogs or a whole can of lentil soup with bread | Make the most of it. This is when you catch up. |
| **Morning, before the dose kicks in** | Cheerios with chocolate milk, or a shake + a banana | Easier to eat before appetite drops. |
| **Low on fibre today** | Half a can of beans (1 min), an apple, or a cup of berries | Beans are the cheapest fibre there is: 11 g for 45¢. |
| **Out of almost everything** | Peanut butter on bread, or a can of chili | Shelf-stable backups. Keep one of each. |

## Tricks that help

- **Set a phone alarm or two** for "drink a shake" or "eat a thing". Appetite won't remind you, so let the phone do it.
- **Keep a shake and a banana right by the bed or the desk.** Food you can see gets eaten.
- **Eat on a schedule, not on hunger.** Even half a portion counts.
- If you're barely eating day after day, mention it to whoever prescribes your meds. They hear it a lot, and they can help.

## Water

- **Aim for about 13 cups (3 L) of drinks a day.** That's the National Academies' typical figure for men, and you're a big guy, so more is fine. Milk, kefir, shakes and soup all count.
- **Stimulants can dull thirst, so don't wait to feel it.**
- **Keep one big bottle where you sit and refill it each time it's empty.** Three or four refills of a 1-litre bottle covers it.
- A glass of water with every food item on this page does most of the work.
- **Quick check: pale yellow pee means you're good. Dark means drink.**
