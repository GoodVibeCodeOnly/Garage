# Three sample days

**Each day hits about 90 g of protein and 30 g of fibre, using only the shopping list.** Times are suggestions, not rules. Eat when you can.

## Day 1: an ordinary day

| When | What | P | F |
|---|---|---|---|
| Morning | Protein shake | 30 | 1 |
| Morning | Cheerios-type 1½ cups + 1 cup chocolate milk | 13 | 5 |
| Midday | Banana | 1 | 3 |
| Afternoon | Two-meat sandwich: 2 oz turkey + 2 oz chicken, 2 slices whole wheat | 32 | 4 |
| Afternoon | Baby carrots, about 10 | 0.5 | 2.5 |
| Evening | Half can black beans, microwaved 1 min, salt + hot sauce | 11 | 11 |
| Evening | Apple | 0.5 | 4.5 |
| Late | Kefir, 1 cup | 10 | 0 |
| | **Total** | **98** | **31** |

## Day 2: a low-appetite day (mostly drinks and small bites)

| When | What | P | F |
|---|---|---|---|
| Morning | Protein shake, sipped slowly | 30 | 1 |
| Midday | Banana | 1 | 3 |
| Afternoon | Kefir 1 cup + 1 cup frozen berries | 11 | 5 |
| Afternoon | Apple | 0.5 | 4.5 |
| Evening (appetite back) | Chili dogs: 2 dogs folded in bread + ½ cup chili | 25 | 7.5 |
| Evening | Rest of that chili cup (½ cup) in a mug | 7 | 3.5 |
| Late | Cheerios-type 1½ cups + 1 cup chocolate milk | 13 | 5 |
| Late | Baby carrots | 0.5 | 2.5 |
| | **Total** | **88** | **32** |

**Close enough.** Add a few bites of chicken anytime and you're over 90.

## Day 3: chicken day (good for the day you shop)

| When | What | P | F |
|---|---|---|---|
| Morning | Chocolate milk 1 cup + a banana | 9 | 4 |
| Midday | Rotisserie chicken, 4 oz, picked straight from the container | 28 | 0 |
| Afternoon | Protein shake | 30 | 1 |
| Evening | Lentil soup, whole can (~2 cups), 2 min, with 2 slices bread to dunk | 24 | 16 |
| Evening | Apple | 0.5 | 4.5 |
| Late | Kefir 1 cup + 1 cup frozen berries | 11 | 5 |
| | **Total** | **102.5** | **30.5** |

**The pattern behind all three days:**

1. One shake a day.
2. One meat thing a day: a sandwich, chicken or hot dogs.
3. One bean thing a day: black beans, chili or lentil soup.
4. Two pieces of fruit.
5. Milk or kefir somewhere.

**Hit those five and you're there without counting anything.**
