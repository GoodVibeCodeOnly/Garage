# 40 near-zero-effort meals and snacks

**Every one takes under two minutes of your time.** The microwave can hum on its own. P = protein, F = fibre, both in grams. Cost is a rough per-serving figure from the [shopping list](shopping-list.md) prices, all estimates. ★ = on the weekly shopping list. Unstarred items are optional swaps for when you have a little extra.

**Microwave rules:** cover it, stir halfway and get it steaming hot.
