# Job search pack

This pack is for reading and deciding. Nothing in it has been sent, posted, or submitted, and nobody has been contacted.

The working assumption, which the evidence in `02-what-gets-people-hired.md` supports: steady entry-level white-collar work is hard to get right now for structural reasons, so this is a problem of positioning and volume, not of personal worth. The pack tries to be specific about where the odds are better and where your background is weak, so you can spend limited energy where it counts.

## Files

| File | What it is |

| `01-role-map.md` | Roles you could realistically get in 3–6 months, ranked by fit and odds, with pay ranges |

## How sourcing works here

Every factual claim about a market, employer or pay rate has a link next to it. Anything I couldn't confirm is marked **UNVERIFIED**. Research was done on 5 Nov 2026; pay rates, open roles and program deadlines change, so check the link before acting on any of them. Pay figures from BLS are for the period stated next to each one, not today.
