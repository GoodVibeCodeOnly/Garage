# Companies and programs to look at

How to read this file: each entry has a link and a note on how the employer hires, with a source for the hiring details. Most details came from search-result text and job aggregators rather than full reads of each employer's site, so **check every posting on the employer's own careers page before you act on it**. Postings found through aggregators may already be closed. Anything marked **UNVERIFIED** I couldn't confirm from a primary source.

Research date: 5 Nov 2026.

## Part 1: Research Triangle employers

### Context first: research employers here have been shrinking

This affects which local targets are worth your time.

- **Duke.** Duke started a hiring freeze, buyouts and layoffs in 2025 in response to federal research cuts. By December 2025 it reported $299M in cuts, and about 599 staff took buyouts. [WITN, Dec 2025](https://www.witn.com/2025/12/29/duke-university-cut-299-million-through-buyouts-building-closures-response-federal-cuts/) · [Higher Ed Dive](https://www.highereddive.com/news/duke-university-buyouts-future-layoffs-trump-administration-cuts-nih/)
- **RTI International.** RTI filed a WARN notice for about 525 permanent layoffs in Durham in 2025. Reporting puts its 2025 workforce reduction at around 35%. [WRAL](https://www.wral.com/21929557) · [EdNC](https://www.ednc.org/the-reality-of-federal-cuts-rti-files-warn-notice-of-mass-layoff/)
- **FHI 360.** It cut 483 US staff, 144 of them in NC, in May 2025 after losing USAID funding. [FHI 360](https://www.fhi360.org/news/fhi-360-announces-reductions-in-force/)
- **UNC-Chapel Hill.** It announced a plan to cut about $70M in 2025. [The Assembly](https://www.theassemblync.com/news/education/higher-education/unc-chapel-hill-unveils-plan-to-cut-70-million/)
- **2026 tech layoffs.** Epic Games cut 211 jobs in Cary in March 2026 ([ABC11](https://abc11.com/post/epic-games-lay-off-more-1000-employees-amid-fortnite-downturn/18762379/)). Pendo cut about 30 jobs in Raleigh in April 2026 ([ABC11](https://abc11.com/post/pendo-layoffs-ai-tech-firm-nc-announces-job-cuts-raleigh-part-company-wide-restructure/18857051/)). The full list is in [NC Commerce's 2026 WARN list](https://www.commerce.nc.gov/report-workforce-warn-summary-list-2026).

My read, which is inference rather than a sourced finding: grant-funded research jobs are a riskier bet than they were before 2025. Laid-off research staff with experience are now competing for the same openings. University temp pools, state and local government, and large finance and operations employers are likely steadier routes in.

### Universities and temp pools (best local odds)

**Duke Temporary Service (DTS)** · https://hr.duke.edu/careers/dts/
- DTS is Duke's in-house temp agency. It staffs both Duke University and Duke Health in clerical/administrative, accounting/finance and IT roles, with part-time and full-time shifts. Temps get no benefits.
- To apply, send a resume through Duke Careers to the standing DTS postings ([example](https://careers.duke.edu/job/Durham-CLERICALADMINISTRATIVE-DUKE-TEMPORARY-SERVICE-NC-27710/1316294000)). The office is at 705 Broad St.
- DTS presents temp work as a way to show your skills toward regular jobs. It does not describe a formal temp-to-hire program. [DTS about page](https://hr.duke.edu/careers/dts/about)
- Pay: a third-party site lists clerical temp pay at $15.50–19.50/hr. **UNVERIFIED.**
- Why it matters for you: once you're inside Duke, you can see internal postings and build references. It's dull work, but it's steady and close to home.

**Duke University / Duke Health regular staff** · https://careers.duke.edu
- Postings show the job code, level and pay range. Examples from current or recent postings:
  - Program Coordinator (level 10): $44,766–$71,706 ([posting](https://careers.duke.edu/job/Durham-Program-Coordinator-NC-27710/1343704600))
  - Program Coordinator, Senior (level 11): $48,960–$77,928 ([posting](https://careers.duke.edu/job/Durham-PROGRAM-COORDINATOR%2C-SENIOR-NC-27710/1352530600))
  - Clinical Research Coordinator (level 52): $59,829–$99,960
- Expect offers near the bottom of each range.
- Duke publishes job descriptions by code ([index](https://forms.hr.duke.edu/descriptions/university/job_code.php?ID=12)). Read them to see which codes accept a BA without specific experience.
- **Duke-Margolis Institute for Health Policy** lists Policy Analyst roles, hybrid Durham/DC: https://healthpolicy.duke.edu/careers. Applications go through Duke Careers.

**UNC-Chapel Hill, University Temporary Services (UTS)** · https://hr.unc.edu/careers/temporary-employment/university-temporary-services
- UTS staffs both UNC and NC State. It keeps standing pools, including a "Temporary Research Pool" and a "Temporary General Talent Pool", and posts specific roles such as Temporary Research Assistant.
- Postings can close within about a week, so check often.
- Regular UNC jobs: Jobs at Carolina (unc.peopleadmin.com).
- The **UNC Sheps Center** (health services research) posts research roles there. https://www.shepscenter.unc.edu/

**NC State** · https://jobs.ncsu.edu
- Aggregators show research assistant postings at $45K–$50K (March 2026) and temporary research assistant pay of about $15/hr. **Pay figures from aggregators, UNVERIFIED.**

**NC Central University.** I didn't research this one. Its own HR jobs site is the place to look. **UNVERIFIED.**

## Part 2: Remote employers, AI platforms and programs

### A. AI evaluation and annotation platforms (fastest to start, least steady)

These platforms are the quickest route to paid AI work that you can list on a resume. None of them is a steady job:

- All of them use 1099 contractor work with no benefits and no guaranteed hours.
- Reviews consistently describe dry spells and projects ending without notice.
- Use them as a bridge and as experience, not as the plan.

**DataAnnotation.tech** · https://dataannotation.tech/faq
- **How they hire:** one unpaid Starter Assessment (about 1 hour; specialist tracks 1–2 hours). You get one attempt, with no retake and no feedback.
- **Pay, as the company states it:** $20+/hr for general work, $40+/hr for coding/STEM, $50+/hr for professional domains ([company blog](https://dataannotation.tech/blog/does-data-annotation-pay-well)).
- **Pay, as independent reviews report it:** $14–20/hr for general tasks ([Second Talent](https://www.secondtalent.com/resources/is-dataannotation-legit/)).
- **Reliability:** reviews from 2026 focus on dry spells in available work.
- **Take it when rested.** There is no second attempt.

**Outlier (run by Scale AI)** · https://outlier.ai/generalists/en
- **Pay, from Outlier's own page:** $12.50–34.50/hr for generalist writing work. Expert pages list higher rates ([personal finance](https://outlier.ai/experts/in-personal-finance)).
- **Reliability:** reviews describe projects stopping abruptly and accounts deactivated without explanation ([Glassdoor, Apr 2026](https://www.glassdoor.com/Reviews/Employee-Review-Outlier-AI-E2858115-RVW103871626.htm)).
- **Lawsuit:** secondary reporting describes a $12.5M settlement of California contributor wage claims (McKinney v. Scale AI). **UNVERIFIED against court records.**
- **Possible fit:** a personal finance or economics expert track may suit your degree. Check what credentials it asks for.

**Mercor** · https://work.mercor.com
- **How they hire:** you upload a resume, then do a role-specific AI video interview of about 20 minutes. Some roles add paid work trials ([interview guide](https://www.techinterview.org/companies/mercor-interview-guide/)).
- **Pay:** listings show $50–100/hr for some tutor roles ([listing](https://work.mercor.com/jobs/list_AAABmBgSuzudqrVqjshClZVs/tutors-educational-services)).
- **Selectivity:** most projects want years of professional experience or an advanced degree.
- **Pay cuts:** secondary reporting says one project's rate was cut from $21 to $16/hr in late 2025 ([WebProNews](https://www.webpronews.com/mercors-ai-gold-rush-billions-in-valuation-pennies-in-pay-cuts)).
- **Odds for you:** lower than DataAnnotation or Outlier.

**Alignerr (Labelbox)** · https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/alignerr
- **How they hire:** some roles use an AI interviewer.
- **Pay:** $15–150/hr depending on role and subject. One data-scientist trainer listing showed $40–80/hr ([listing](https://catalitium.com/jobs/136140-alignerr-hiring-data-scientist-masters-ai-data-trainer)).
- **Reliability:** reviews report pending pay lost when accounts were closed ([annotation.academy](https://annotation.academy/blog/alignerr-opinions)). **Secondary source.**

**Invisible Technologies** · https://job-boards.eu.greenhouse.io/invisibletech
- **How they hire:** a timed English test, then writing and AI-text assessments, then an AI video interview. Failed assessments get no feedback ([Glassdoor interviews](https://www.glassdoor.ca/Interview/Invisible-Technologies-Interview-E2342977-RVW89321539.htm)).
- **Fit:** writing-heavy, which suits you.

**Handshake AI (MOVE / Fellowship)** · [Handshake help article](https://support.joinhandshake.com/hc/en-us/articles/31620022060055-Introduction-to-the-MOVE-Program)
- **Terms:** 1099 work, typically 5–20 hrs/week, on projects that run from 2 weeks to months.
- **Catch:** being accepted to the fellowship doesn't mean you'll get paid work. You need a project invitation, and expert-level roles seem to dominate.

**Turing.** Profile, then automated tests, then an AI interview. US AI-trainer listings reportedly pay about $25–55/hr ([talentsforai](https://talentsforai.com/blog/is-turing-legit/)). **No official Turing source found; UNVERIFIED.**

**Surge AI.** **UNVERIFIED.** I found no hiring details.

### B. AI companies: realistic and not

The honest picture:

- Most support, solutions, and technical-writing roles at well-known AI labs ask for 5–8+ years or require office attendance.
- They're worth watching, and one application each costs little.
- They shouldn't be where most of your energy goes this quarter.

**Anthropic** · https://www.anthropic.com/careers
- US postings require being in an office at least 25% of the time.
- Support Operations Specialist is listed as in-office at about $131–165K ([Built In](https://builtin.com/job/support-operations-specialist/7684402)).
- **Low odds and not remote.**

**OpenAI**
- US-remote AI Success Engineer and AI Support Engineer roles ask for 8+ years ([listing](https://jobgether.com/offer/69725f4b0365b76b0abbd007-ai-success-engineer---us-remote)).
- **Not realistic yet.**

**Cohere**
- Senior Technical Writer (US remote) asks for 5+ years ([listing](https://www.remoterocketship.com/company/cohere/jobs/senior-technical-writer-united-states-remote)).
- **Not realistic yet.**

**Glean**
- Designated AI Support Engineer, US remote, mid-level, posted Apr 2026 ([listing](https://aplyr.ai/jobs/0077cf08690c/designated-ai-support-engineer)).
- A stretch, but closer to your experience than the labs' roles.

**Zapier** · https://zapier.com/jobs
- Fully remote.
- **How they hire:** a hiring-manager call, then a skills interview (a short take-home plus a live call), then a values interview. About 4–6 weeks, and they say they aim never to go more than 7 days without contact ([Zapier](https://zapier.com/de/jobs/our-commitment-to-applicants)).
- Automation and AI-ops work fits your profile. **One of the better remote targets here.**

**Hugging Face** · https://builtin.com/company/hugging-face/jobs
- Many roles are tagged US-remote, but most are engineering or developer relations.
- Watch for community, documentation, or support openings.

**Sierra** · https://sierra.ai/careers
- Mostly in-office in major cities ([listing](https://www.hirebase.org/company/sierra/jobs/agent-engineer-1)). **Not remote.**

**Decagon, Intercom, LangChain.** **UNVERIFIED.** Not enough was found to say how they hire.

**A broader point:** the AI-implementation openings you're most likely to land are at **ordinary software companies adding AI features**, not at AI labs. Search job boards for "AI implementation", "AI operations", "AI enablement", "solutions consultant" and "implementation specialist". Read the requirements, not the titles.

- Research analyst roles ask for a BA plus 3+ years of research or policy work. DC-based. **Remote eligibility UNVERIFIED.**
