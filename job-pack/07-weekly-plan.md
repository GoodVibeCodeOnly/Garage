# Weekly plan (one page)

This plan assumes irregular hours and that some weeks will be low-energy. It sets a floor that works on a bad week and a fuller version for normal weeks. Hitting the floor counts as a successful week. The schedule is in blocks, not time slots, so they can happen at 2 PM or 2 AM.

## The floor (bad weeks, about 2–3 hours total)

1. **Two targeted applications** to roles from the top tiers in `01-role-map.md`. Tailor the resume summary and one cover letter paragraph, nothing more.
2. **One follow-up or one contact.** Answer a recruiter, reply to a thread, or send one short note to someone you already know.
3. **Update the tracker** (below). Five minutes.

## The normal week (about 8–12 hours)

| Block | Count per week | What |
|---|---|---|
| Applications | 5–8 | Tiers 1–2 first. Skip roles where you meet under about half the hard requirements. |
| Contract/platform pipeline | 1 | Keep one evaluation/annotation platform application or assessment moving (see `03-companies-and-programs.md`). It's the quickest route to income that also counts as experience. |
| Portfolio | 1–2 blocks of 2 hours | Next step in `06-portfolio-plan.md`, in order. |
| Python | 3 × 45 minutes | Write by hand, then use AI to review. Aim to be able to pass a basic live coding screen. |
| People | 2 | One message to someone in a target role or org (ask a specific question, not for a job), and one local or online event/meetup per month. |
| Review | 20 minutes | Tracker review: what got responses, what didn't, and adjust the target list. |

## Tracker (a spreadsheet is enough)

Columns: date · company · role · tier · link · resume variant · cover letter (A/B/C) · referral? (y/n) · status · next action · next action date.

After 30 applications, look at the response rate by tier and by variant. If one tier gets zero responses after about 15 applications, the problem is more likely fit or materials than effort; shift weight toward the tiers that are responding.

## Rules that save energy

- Batch tailoring. Write cover letters for three similar roles in one sitting.
- Don't apply to roles posted more than about 3 weeks ago unless they're at a target employer.
- Don't take unpaid "trial projects" longer than about 2 hours. Paid work samples are fine.
- Treat platform assessments (evaluation work) as a lottery ticket with a short expiry: do them when you have energy, and don't wait on a result.
- If a week is completely missed, restart at the floor. Don't try to make up the lost week.

## Month markers (check, don't stress)

- **End of month 1:** resume variants final, platemail.gg case study published, 15–25 applications out, at least one platform assessment taken.
- **End of month 2:** evaluation write-up published, 35–50 applications total, first interviews likely if materials are working.
- **Month 3:** if there have been no interviews, revisit the target list and the resume with someone in the field before sending more. Volume isn't the fix at that point.
