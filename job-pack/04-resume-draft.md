# Resume draft

Everything in `[brackets]` is something this pack doesn't know and must not guess. Fill it in or delete the line. Nothing here has been sent anywhere.

Format notes, so the file survives applicant tracking systems: single column, no tables, no icons, plain section headings, save as .docx and PDF, file name `Lastname_Firstname_Resume.pdf`. Keep it to one page until there is a second page's worth of paid work to show.

The structure leads with projects, not employment history, because the projects are the strongest evidence you have. That is a deliberate choice, and it is the usual advice for career changers, but it does make a reviewer look harder at the work section. Leaving the work section out is worse: unexplained gaps get filtered.

## Variants to keep

Keep one master copy and make three short variants. Only the summary line and the order of the bullets change; facts never change.

| Variant | Summary leads with | Move to the top |
|---|---|---|
| AI ops / evaluation / data | "evaluates and operates AI systems" | Measurement work, then multi-agent system |
| Implementation / solutions / support | "builds AI products for real users" | platemail.gg, then client demo |
| Writing / research / policy | "analyst and writer" | Thesis or writing samples, then measurement write-up |

## Where this resume is weak, plainly

- **No employer has vouched for the AI work yet.** Self-directed projects are discounted unless the reviewer can see them. The portfolio plan (`05-portfolio-plan.md`) exists to fix this.
- **"Learning Python" will be tested.** Many AI-adjacent roles include a coding screen or a take-home. Claim only the level you can perform live, without AI help, in a timed setting.
- **Directing AI coding is not yet a recognized credential.** Phrase it as outcomes (what got built and shipped), not as a skill label.
- **The work history is the first thing a screener will question.** Answer it in one calm line in a cover letter or interview, not by padding the resume.
