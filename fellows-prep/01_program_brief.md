## The program in general
- **Format:** 4 months, full-time, empirical research with mentorship from Anthropic researchers. The aim is a public output, such as a paper. Sources: [alignment.anthropic.com: Fellows 2026](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2025/anthropic-fellows-program-2026/), [Built In summary](https://builtin.com/articles/anthropic-fellows-program)
- **Stipend:** "expected base stipend of 3,850 USD / 2,310 GBP / 4,300 CAD per week." Source: [posting mirror (Index Ventures)](https://www.indexventures.com/startup-jobs/anthropic/anthropic-fellows-program-the-anthropic-institute-economics-policy/)
- **Compute and research budget:** about $15k/month. Sources: [Extern guide](https://www.extern.com/post/anthropic-fellows-program-guide). ⚠ I couldn't confirm whether this applies the same way to the Economics & Policy track.
- **Eligibility:** full-time work authorisation in the US, UK or Canada, and you must be located there during the program. They are "not currently able to sponsor visas." Sources: [posting mirror (Accel)](https://jobs.accel.com/companies/anthropic/jobs/74068663-anthropic-fellows-program). The Berkeley workspace is run by Constellation.
- **Background:** prior safety or economics research is "not required." One line covers all workstreams: "you may be a good fit if you are motivated by making sure AI is safe and beneficial for society as a whole." Source: [Greenhouse posting](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/anthropic/jobs/5023394008)
- **Tracks:** each has its own posting, and you should "only apply to the one that best matches." They are:
  - AI Safety (with Security)
  - ML Systems & Performance
  - Reinforcement Learning
  - **The Anthropic Institute (Economics & Policy)**

  Sources: [ML Systems](https://jobs.accel.com/companies/anthropic/jobs/80247014-anthropic-fellows-program-ml-systems-performance), [RL](https://jobs.generalcatalyst.com/companies/anthropic/jobs/79778960-anthropic-fellows-program-reinforcement-learning), [Econ & Policy](https://jobs.generalcatalyst.com/companies/anthropic/jobs/76935323-anthropic-fellows-program-the-anthropic-institute-fellows-economics-policy)
- **Selection stages (official posting, via snippet):**
  1. Application and reference check
  2. Technical assessments and interviews
  3. Research discussion
  4. Project selection and mentor matching

  Source: [Greenhouse](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/anthropic/jobs/5183052008). Unofficial accounts, mostly about the *Safety* track, describe a timed coding test of about 90 minutes with no AI tools, a live coding round, and a research brainstorm. These accounts disagree on details. Sources: [IGotAnOffer](https://igotanoffer.com/en/advice/anthropic-ai-safety-fellow-interview), [Aced](https://www.aced.io/guides/anthropic-ai-safety-fellow-interview). ⚠ I found **nothing** on whether Economics & Policy applicants take a coding test.

## Economics & Policy track ("The Anthropic Institute")
- **Posting title:** "Anthropic Fellows Program, The Anthropic Institute (Economics & Policy)." Source: [Greenhouse](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/anthropic/jobs/5183053008)
- **Materials requested:**
  - "**a writing sample, preferably a job-market paper or other article that showcases your research and technical expertise**"
  - CV
  - mentor preferences
  - website or LinkedIn
  - a short "why Anthropic"
  - references

  Sources: [Greenhouse](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/anthropic/jobs/5183053008), [Daybook mirror](https://www.daybook.com/job/anthropic-fellows-program-the-anthropic-institute-economics-policy-QxATNzKAvfLCWyeFC)
- **Looking for:** "interest in economics or policy research; prior experience in this area is a plus but not required." The posting also mentions communicating findings clearly *including when results are inconclusive or unexpected*. Source: [General Catalyst mirror](https://jobs.generalcatalyst.com/companies/anthropic/jobs/76935323-anthropic-fellows-program-the-anthropic-institute-fellows-economics-policy)
- ⚠ **Who runs the process:** an older mirror says Constellation runs applications and interviews. A newer guide says Anthropic runs Economics & Policy directly.
- ⚠ **What "The Anthropic Institute" is** as an organisation: I didn't verify this. I only saw the name in the posting title.
- **Related but separate:**
  - [Economic Futures Program](https://anthropic.com/economic-futures/program), launched June 2025, with research awards of about $10k–$50k. It's a possible smaller first step that doesn't require a full-time commitment.
  - ⚠ A reported June 2026 expansion of $350M ($200M research fund plus the $150M "Claude Corps" fellowship). This is secondary-source only: [ai2.work](https://ai2.work/blog/anthropic-bets-350m-on-studying-ai-s-job-market-fallout). Claude Corps is a *different*, 12-month early-career program, which is easy to confuse with this one.

## What past fellows produced
- **Publication and hiring rates:** first cohort, "over 80% of fellows produced papers" and "over 40% … joined Anthropic full-time." A later version says "over 65% … published." Source: [alignment.anthropic.com](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2025/anthropic-fellows-program-2026/)
- **Named outputs** (all on the safety side):
  - subliminal learning ([arXiv 2507.21509](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.21509))
  - agentic misalignment
  - persona vectors
  - Petri
  - open-source circuits
  - rapid response to jailbreaks
- ⚠ **I found no published outputs from Economics & Policy fellows.** The track looks newer. The nearest public model of "what good looks like" is Anthropic's own [Economic Index](https://www.anthropic.com/economic-index) reports and their public dataset.

## So which work samples matter (inference, labelled as such)
The posting asks for *one* writing sample, "preferably a job-market paper or other article." A non-PhD applicant can't produce a job-market paper. The realistic substitute, in my judgement, is a short, finished, public empirical paper that has:
1. A clear question.
2. Real data, with the code public.
3. Honest robustness and limitations sections.
4. Ideally, use of Anthropic's own Economic Index data.

The posting's own emphasis on reporting "inconclusive or unexpected" results suggests a well-handled null is acceptable. `04_first_pass_writeup.md` is a seed for such a sample, not the sample itself.
